# Account Access and Security Guide

## Account Recovery and Password Reset
If you have forgotten your password, follow these recovery steps:
1. Navigate to the login screen and click the "Forgot Password" link below the credentials form.
2. Enter your registered email address and click "Send Recovery Link".
3. Check your email for an automated message containing a password reset link. This link is only valid for 24 hours.
4. Click the link, choose a new strong password (minimum 8 characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character), and submit.
If you do not receive the email, please check your spam/junk folder or verify if you entered the correct email address.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
To secure your account, we recommend enabling 2FA:
1. Log in to your account and click on your profile picture in the top-right corner, then select Profile Settings.
2. Navigate to the Security tab and click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication".
3. Use a mobile authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Duo, or Authy) to scan the displayed QR code.
4. Enter the 6-digit verification code generated by the app to confirm setup.
Once enabled, you will be prompted to enter a 2FA code during every login attempt. If you lose your authenticator device, you can use the backup recovery codes generated during the setup process to bypass the login prompt.

## Account Lockouts
To prevent unauthorized access, our security system automatically locks an account after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
- The lockout duration is 30 minutes. After 30 minutes, the account is unlocked automatically, and you can attempt to log in again.
- If you need to access your account immediately or unlock it before the 30-minute window ends, click the "Unlock Account" link in the lockout warning email or contact our support team at support@saascompany.com.

## Updating Profile Information
To update your username, primary email address, or contact details, navigate to Profile Settings from your account dashboard. Changing your primary email address will require you to verify the new email address via a confirmation link sent to that inbox before the change takes effect.
